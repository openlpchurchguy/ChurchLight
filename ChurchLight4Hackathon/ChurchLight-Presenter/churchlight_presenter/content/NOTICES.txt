King James Version (bibles/kjv.csv)
-----------------------------------
Text:     The King James (Authorized) Version of the Holy Bible, the standardized text of 1769,
          66 books (no Apocrypha), 31,102 verses.
Source:   eBible.org, "King James (Authorized) Version", id eng-kjv2006,
          https://ebible.org/find/show.php?id=eng-kjv2006 (file eng-kjv2006_vpl.zip, downloaded 2026-10-26;
          the eBible archive is dated 2026-09-26). eBible.org credits the CrossWire Bible Society.
License:  Public domain in the United States and most of the world.
          In the United Kingdom, letters patent issued by King James give the Cambridge University Press,
          the Oxford University Press and Collins the exclusive right to print this translation there;
          printing it in, or importing printed copies into, the UK needs their permission. Please see
          the Queen's (King's) Printer patent information if you distribute the program in the UK.
Changes:  made with tools/make_kjv.py and nothing else: book codes became book numbers, the paragraph
          sign was removed, extra spaces were squeezed. The words are unchanged. kjv.sha256 holds the
          checksum of kjv.csv; the program installs the file only if it matches.

No other translation (NIV, ESV, NLT, NKJV, NASB and so on) is bundled; those are under copyright.
