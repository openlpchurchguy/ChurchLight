Bringing your slides into ChurchLight

ChurchLight takes a PDF (or slide pictures). Pick the program you made your slides in.

  1. In PowerPoint, press 16:9 first if needed: Design, then Slide Size, then Widescreen (16:9).
  2. Choose File, then Export (or Save As), then PDF. On Windows this is Create PDF/XPS Document. On a Mac choose Export, then PDF as the file format.
  3. Name it and press Publish (or Save or Export). Look in the options that no hidden slides are left out if you want them.
  4. In ChurchLight, go to the Slideshow Library and press Choose a PDF…, then pick the file you just saved.
  1. In Google Slides, check the shape: File, then Page setup, then Widescreen 16:9.
  2. Choose File, then Download, then PDF Document (.pdf). The file goes to your Downloads folder.
  3. In ChurchLight, go to the Slideshow Library and press Choose a PDF…, then pick that file.

The PDF has your slides only. Speaker notes are not in it, so type them into ChurchLight (see below).

  1. In Keynote, choose File, then Export To, then PDF.
  2. Leave the options as they are (each slide gets its own page), press Next, then name it and press Export.
  3. In ChurchLight, go to the Slideshow Library and press Choose a PDF…, then pick that file.
  1. In Impress, choose File, then Export As, then Export as PDF….
  2. Leave the options as they are and press Export, then name it and save.
  3. In ChurchLight, go to the Slideshow Library and press Choose a PDF…, then pick that file.

What does not come across, and what to do

  • Speaker notes. A PDF does not carry them. Open the slideshow in the slideshow editor and type each page's notes into its Notes box.
  • Animations, builds and transitions. Every page becomes one still picture. Use ChurchLight's own transition and timing for the slideshow instead.
  • Videos and sound inside a slide. They are not kept. Add them in the Media Library and use them next to the slideshow.
  • Hidden slides. They are usually left out. Show them again in your slide program before you export if you want them.
  • Slide shape. Set 16:9 before you export so the pictures fill the screen with no side bars.
  • Fonts and colours. These are kept, because the pages are pictures of your slides.
  • Size. Very large PDFs can take a while. If one is refused, export again with the smaller or standard size option.
  • No PDF option? Export the slides as pictures (PNG or JPG) instead, then press Choose slide pictures…. Pictures in order of their file names become the pages.

                              Media Library

                              Pictures, videos and sound for your services. Add a file once, then use it as a service item, as music under the slides, or as a page in a slideshow (in the slideshow editor, Add from Media Library). Videos are MP4 (H.264); sound is MP3 or WAV.

                              How to prepare a video (and what to do if one will not play)

                              ChurchLight, OBS and XSplit play video most reliably as an MP4 with H.264 video and AAC sound, at 1080p or smaller and 30 frames a second or less. Phones, cameras and screen recorders sometimes save in newer formats (H.265, HEVC, 10-bit, 4K or 60 frames a second) that look fine in a media player but freeze or stay black here.

                              To re-save a video so it always works (this is outside ChurchLight and free):

                              1. Install HandBrake from handbrake.fr.
                              2. Open HandBrake and drag your video into it.
                              3. Under Preset, choose Fast 1080p30.
                              4. Check that Format says MP4.
                              5. Choose where to save it, press Start, and wait for it to finish.
                              6. Add the new file here. You can delete the old one from this list.

                              If a video is already playing badly: press Clear live, remove that video from the list below, and add the re-saved copy. Sound files can be MP3 or WAV.

                                  How verses look

                                  This look is used for every verse on the projector, and the way it splits long verses (characters per slide) applies to all screens. Make or edit themes on the Themes tab. A theme picked on one item wins.

                                  A verse shown over a slideshow can have a look of its own on the projector: choose an Overlay theme above to set its box (position, width, band) and text, as for a lower third. The lower third keeps its own theme. Leave it on "Same as the Bible theme" to keep the Bible look. To keep these verses off the projector altogether, tick "Lower third only" in that theme on the Themes tab.

                                    Projector windows

                                    ChurchLight can open its own full-screen windows on your projector, stage and confidence monitors (Windows with Edge or Chrome).                                       Screens

                                      Home screen

                                      A picture the projector shows when nothing else should be on screen: while nothing is live (no service open, or before the first slide) and, if you like, while the screens are blanked, for example between parts of the service. Press Blank to show it and again to go back. With no picture, the projector shows nothing. The lower third and other screens are not affected.

                                      Hide words

                                      The Hide words button (W) takes the words off the projector and keeps the background. Choose whether the confidence monitor loses its words too. The stage screens always keep theirs.

                                      Sound

                                      Video and music play from this ChurchLight window, on this computer, so the room hears them through the computer's speakers. To put the sound on the stream, add "Desktop Audio" in OBS (it is there by default). Untick this if another window on this computer already plays the sound.

                                      Screen shape

                                      The shape of the real projector and confidence monitor, so the previews match what the room sees. Set the XSplit or OBS source to the same size.

                                        Screen addresses

                                        Paste an address into an OBS or XSplit web page source (a "browser source" in OBS) to show that screen. Use the first list on this computer. The second list, when it appears, is for another computer on your network.

                                            Remote button links

                                            Links that press a button when opened (also called Trigger URLs), for a Stream Deck, XSplit or any program that can open a web address. They work on this computer only. Each link includes a private code, so only copy it into your own tools and do not post it anywhere; if it gets out, make a new private code.

                                              Clicker helper (Windows)

                                              A presentation clicker types ordinary keys (Page Down, Page Up and so on). The helper catches those keys and sends only a remote button link, so the clicker moves ChurchLight and nothing else on the computer sees the key. ChurchLight comes with the free AutoHotkey program it needs, so there is nothing to install.

                                                1. Press Press the button, then press that button on the clicker. Choose what it should do.
                                                2. Press Start the clicker helper. A message says the clicker is ready. If you change the buttons afterwards, press it again (it then says Restart).
                                                3. Ctrl+Alt+F12 turns the clicker on and off. If ChurchLight is not running, the keys work normally again.
                                                4. Making a new private code (Remote button links, above) restarts the helper by itself. Turning Remote button links off stops it.

                                                The script contains the private code from the Remote button links above, so keep it on this computer and do not send it around. After you make a new private code, download the script again.

                                                Keys for running the service

                                                These keys move the service along while this window is in front and you are not typing in a box. They work on the Service and Alerts Settings tabs. Press Change next to a job, then press the key you want. Your choices are kept in this browser.

                                                Keys that work in any program

                                                For a clicker or keyboard when another program, such as XSplit, is in front. Windows only. Off until you turn them on. While on, the keys below are taken from every program on this computer so a clicker or keyboard can move the service from anywhere. Turn them off and the keys go straight back. Only the keys you choose here are used, and they are never turned on by themselves when the program starts.
